Reading Reflection 1

Photography has become a huge part of my life; every day, I take pictures of my life. I love to take pictures to capture the moment. Whether it’s a selfie to remember what I looked like that day or capture a sweet moment between friends, I take photos as an extra reminder of that moment. There are many memories I have forgotten, and photography acts as an aid to refresh my memory. The best kind of feeling is seeing a photo from the years of my childhood, and the wave of nostalgia hits me. It’s a feeling of “omg I forgot this happened” and a gratefulness to that photo, which was taken.

Imagine making has also become a huge part of my life since I’ve become active on social media. Before posting on Instagram, I feel the responsibility to choose the best photo and edit it to instagram worthy level. I love being able to edit the angle, color, and crop my image to my liking. I am able to focus on a certain aesthetic that I am trying to portray when I edit my photos. For example, when I take photos of the city skylines, I edit the contrast a lot to portray a deeper and richer city, or when I take photos of the flowers in the park, I usually edit the brightest to look more alive and vibrant. I love image making because I can add my personal touch, and the photo reflects my personality in the image.

The world at this moment of my life is very conflicted due to the issues of the government and politics, but despite this, people have been coming together becoming stronger to help empower the communities that are affected. To visually represent this, I would create an image collage of multiple protests happening throughout the world for each situation. For example, for the protest against the war between Palestine and Israel, I would find images of these protests and create a side by side collage. I would also include the dates of these protests to show how the world can come together on the same or similar dates despite being in different parts of the world. I would focus on the feeling of connection and collectiveness in these photos, highlighting similarity with the groups in the photos. The intention of this art piece would be to represent the amount of support people have for each other. The piece would be named “No one is Alone,” to remind people that their efforts are being seen and to show there's a lot more support than we think. I believe that artwork like this uplifts people in times of pain and suffering.

I believe with the right tools and mindset, photography and image making can be used as a great tool to connect people with multiple experiences. These art forms have been used for a long time to tell stories of those who have come before, playing a huge role in the history of the world.

Reading Reflection 2

This article explores an emerging issue involving the use of AI, which is changing our perception of reality. The author refers to this concept as “AI slop,” in which there is a creation of a mix of our real issues and experiences and AI-generated ideas. With the current advancements in our technology, it is becoming difficult to tell what is real and not through imagery and videos. This becomes an issue particularly when serious topics, such as politics or news, get into the mix, because people struggle to decipher the truth. On the one hand, generative AI does give people a new ability to create fun and silly videos, but on the other hand, people use it to create false narratives, which distort people's perception of what's real and fake. AI content plus big platforms like instagram or whatsapp heightens the amount of people who this consent is able to reach with ease. A huge concern with this type of AI is the usage creating false narratives. An example is how they have used AI to create a "political fantasy” in which they depict fake stories of politicians causing harm to certain groups, which not only harms the group that is targeted but also enables the people who agree with the depiction, causing not only more delusion but also giving support to this genre of content. AI has become a propaganda tool and an efficient one at that. AI is not only easily accessible, but it’s also easy to spread, which causes more roadblocks in combating this issue. Another issue that commonly occurs with AI is the differentiation between reality and fiction. AI has improved its quality quickly over the years, and now I struggle to tell the difference anymore. For people who are older or not so acquainted with the use of AI, it has become impossible to decipher reality for some, and if no one tells them it's fake, to them, what they just saw was real and true. This issue heightens when false propaganda is created, subconsciously putting harmful ideas in people’s minds. Lastly, an overall issue is the neurological impact these videos have on our brains. When AI creations make fun of very serious situations going on in the world, such as wars or human rights being violated it lessens the severity of the problem. A goofy depiction of a serious issue can confuse the mind about what is really happening in our world. As the author says, people are "sleepwalking into disaster" with AI becoming a distraction of destruction. Unfortunately, AI has been incorporated into our lives with more prevalence than ever. It's impossible to avoid it if you use the internet. In regards to this article, I am happy that awareness of the harm AI is causing is being spread. I think AI can be an effective tool to create positive experiences. We need more regulations and laws regarding the usage of AI. There should definitely be a mandatory label with anything that uses AI and restrictions on the type of content people are able to create. This article was insightful and necessary to our current situation.

Reading Reflection 3

In “Memunatu: The Distant Patient – Birth in my village,” Memunatu’s story highlights the challenges of childbirth in a village setting where she was wrongfully mistreated by a false nurse. Her experience reflects the struggles many women face when giving birth without immediate professional support. Memunatu’s situation shows the importance of having a proper healthcare system in reach of people. She fell ill after the birthing incident and had to travel to a hospital for the first time. If there were more hospitals in her area the first incident wouldn't have occurred. This story brings attention to cultural and environmental issues women have to face. The structure of the story was really easy to follow and the images added a lot of depth to the story especially the part where they added real images of the situation happening. The ending where she was able to deliver a healthy baby concluded the story well and added emphasis to the story’s message. Overall, Memunatu’s story is powerful because it illustrates the reality of health disparities. It exemplifies why improving healthcare access in rural communities is essential and important.
